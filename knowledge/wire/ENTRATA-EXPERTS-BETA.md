## Summary

Entrata Experts (Analysts + Assistants) is live and customer interest is strong. Before scaling to General Availability, we're running a structured 2-month closed Beta with 5 curated customers to validate the product with real usage data, identify friction points, and build the testimonials needed for a strong GA launch.

FP&A has approved **$12,700 in Beta credits** to fund this program.

---

## Beta Investment

| Parameter | Value |

## What We Get in Return

Customers receive free credits. We receive something more valuable:

- **Real usage data:** actual questions asked, frequency, and patterns
- **Error identification:** where the system fails or underperforms
- **Product signal:** what features drive the most value per product
- **Engagement sessions:** scheduled feedback calls with each customer

The $12,700 investment de-risks a product going to thousands of properties. The cost of not doing this is measured in adoption rate, churn risk, and a weaker GA story.

---

## Why a Beta Before GA

Entrata Experts is live, and customer interest is strong. Before we scale to General Availability, we need real-world validation: actual usage data, real questions asked by real teams, and the friction points we can only discover in production.

A structured, free Beta with a curated set of customers gives us exactly that — and gives us the testimonials and proof points we need to win at GA.
